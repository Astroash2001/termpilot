How TermPilot works
ON
The status capsule
The black capsule at the top says whether the phone reaches your PC's terminal: Connected, Offline or PC offline. Below it is what you last sent.
Voice prompts
Tap the mic and speak. Your words are added to whatever is already in the prompt box.
History and menus
Swipe across the prompt box to recall past prompts. The up and down keys move through menus in the terminal, or through history while you are typing.
Commands for any agent
Start Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Aider, agy or any other agent in the terminal. TermPilot spots which one is running, and / or / CMD shows its commands.
Key row
^C interrupts, ESC leaves a menu, CLR clears the screen, TAB accepts the agent's suggestion or completes a path. To copy text, long-press the terminal or tap the copy button next to the text size.
The desk PC
The little PC in the bubble under the screen's bottom-right corner mirrors your computer: a key flashes as you type and its screen lights when output arrives. Tap it to turn the completion chime off or on.
Fits your phone
The terminal on your PC resizes to your phone's screen, so lines wrap to fit instead of running off the edge. A− A+ change the text size and the lines re-wrap. Type on the PC and it takes its own size back; type on the phone and the phone gets it again.
